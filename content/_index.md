**Synergy VR** is a highly engineered, comprehensive overhaul for Skyrim VR built on a simple philosophy: stability, performance, and maximum immersion. True to its name, Synergy ensures hundreds of deeply integrated systems for combat, lighting, weather, audio, and physics work in perfect harmony to deliver a seamless, high-end "Vanilla Enhanced" world.

## Standout Features & Highlights

### Tactile VR Physics & Real-Time Collision

- **Physical Combat & Parrying**: Real-time melee collisions via Physical Collision VR and Precision Physic Bodies, physical weapon parrying, and real-time object throwing. (Durability VR ships in the optional folder if you want your gear to wear with use.)
- **Traversal & Body Mechanics**: Physically scale mountain faces, city walls, and structural ruins with VR Climbing, watch real-time ragdoll physics apply to all NPCs, animals, and massive dragons, and sever limbs with the integrated Dismembering Framework.
- **Reactive Geometry**: The environment responds to your hands and weapons with physics-based doors and swinging chains.
- **Secondary Physics**: Full HDT-SMP and CBPC frameworks ensure fluid hair, clothing, and armor movement.

### Visual Depth, Lighting & Weather Pipeline

- **Bespoke Weather & ENB**: A choice of custom VR-optimized ENB presets over NAT.ENB III weathers, delivering crisp daytime contrast, vibrant color accuracy, and deep, realistic nights that reward torch and light spell gameplay.
- **Full Lux Integration**: Fully integrated Lux, Lux Orbis, and Lux Variants for realistic, hand-crafted interior and exterior lighting, alongside Crossroads of Skyrim.
- **Advanced Landscape Depth**: Smart mesh layering on mountains, dirt cliffs, roads, and shorelines, with BENDr, Auto Parallax, and VR Parallax Shader Fix working around VR's terrain limitations to create a striking optical illusion of physical depth and texture variation.
- **Foliage & World Textures**: High-fidelity ground cover and terrain via Atlantean and Riton Landscapes, Folkvangr, and Tamrielic Grass.

### True Binaural Spatial Audio

- **HRTF Spatial Accuracy**: Powered by Binaural 3D Surround Sound (HRTF). Track dragons, spell trajectories, or sneaking enemies purely by ear with pinpoint 360-degree directional tracking.
- **Acoustic Fingerprints**: Acoustic Space Improvement Fixes simulates real-world physics. Massive stone dungeons, cramped wooden taverns, and damp caves each possess a distinctly modeled echo and reverb profile.
- **Immersive Soundscapes**: Regional Sounds Expansion, Immersive Sounds - Compendium, and Lucidity Sound FX replace generic ambient loops with dynamic, location-specific nature, weather, and environmental audio.

### Balanced Gameplay & Slow-Burn Economy

- **Systemic Consistency**: Built on the Simonrim suite (Adamant, Mysticism, Mundus, Thaumaturgy, Manbeast) alongside Sacrosanct vampires for cohesive perk, magic, and progression balance.
- **Tactical Melee Combat**: Features Blade and Blunt VR, Accuracy (Localized Combat Damage), SkyTactics, Strike Obstruction Systems, and Dual Wield Block VR.
- **Progression & Scarcity**: Features the Experience framework alongside SkyPatcher dynamics scaling back high-tier loot via Scarcity SE and Trade and Barter.

### VR Engineering & Clean Architecture

- **Engine Optimization**: Tools like eFPS, Disk Cache Enabler, Private Profile Redirector, and Engine Fixes VR (plus a prebuilt VRAMr texture pass) keep the experience smooth and stutter-free.
- **Industry-Leading MO2 Layout**: Built with a hyper-organized, color-coded structure of 100+ categories, complete with a step-by-step setup guide right inside Mod Organizer 2.
- **Plug-and-Play Setup**: Automates initial configuration via an MCM Recorder profile, and offers native, dead-simple OpenComposite vs. SteamVR runtime toggles.
